GHOST TATTOO

About me

Hi, I'm Dejan

I was born in Rijeka where I have had my Ghost tattoo studio for a long time. I have always loved art and the first trigger was graffiti, and later I gradually entered the tattoo world. It started as a hobby, and later grew into a great passion and life calling. I have gained experience all over Europe, and also participated and won awards at major conventions. I am not limited to a specific style, but my favorites are realism, pop art, fine line, trash polka and all combinations related to these styles. I usually do one tattoo a day because I believe that every story and desire deserves full attention and emotion that will remain etched in memory, just like on the skin.
