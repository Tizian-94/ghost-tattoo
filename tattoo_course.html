Individual and Group Tattoo Courses – All Year Round!

I offer individual courses, as well as group workshops (for multiple people) held several times a month throughout the year. These courses are ideal for beginners, but also for those who want to improve their skills.

About the Course

  • ✅ You will learn everything – from the basics of tattooing to advanced techniques, with practical work and mentoring.
  • ✅ Faster progress than with YouTube – because you have professional support and are getting immediate answers to your questions.
  • ✅ With this course you don't just buy knowledge – you buy time and accelerate your path to becoming a professional tattoo artist.
  • Invest in yourself and your knowledge – become a top tattoo artist with proper training!

Individualni i grupni tečajevi tetoviranja – kroz cijelu godinu!

Nudim individualne tečajeve, kao i grupne radionice (za više osoba) koje se održavaju nekoliko puta mjesečno tijekom cijele godine. Ovi tečajevi su idealni za početnike, ali i za one koji žele unaprijediti svoje vještine.

O tečaju

  • ✅ Naučit ćeš sve – od osnova tetoviranja do naprednih tehnika, uz praktičan rad i mentorstvo.
  • ✅ Brži napredak nego uz YouTube – jer imaš stručnu podršku i odmah dobivaš odgovore na pitanja.
  • ✅ S ovim tečajem ne kupuješ samo znanje – kupuješ vrijeme i ubrzavaš svoj put do toga da postaneš profesionalni tattoo artist.
  • Uloži u sebe i svoje znanje – postani vrhunski tattoo umjetnik uz pravi trening!
